Principal's Message

A note from our Principal

Dr. Meera Krishnan on learning, character and partnering with families.

26 YearsExperience

Dear Parents, Students and Friends of EDUCARE,

It is my privilege to welcome you to EDUCARE International School. For twenty-five years, our school has stood for one simple idea: every child carries a spark, and our job is to help it shine.

In a world transformed by technology, knowledge alone is no longer enough. Our students must learn to think critically, collaborate across cultures, communicate with empathy and adapt with resilience. That is why our classrooms are places of inquiry — where students design experiments, debate ideas, build robots, write poetry and lead community projects.

Education is not a race to the finish line. It is a journey of discovering who you are and what you can give to the world.

Partnership with families

Parents are our most important partners. Through our real-time parent portal, regular conversations and open-door policy, we ensure you are always part of your child's journey — from attendance and homework to achievements and wellbeing.

Our promise

  • Every child will be known by name, strengths and dreams.
  • Every classroom will be safe, inclusive and joyful.
  • Every teacher will keep learning, just like our students.
  • Every family will be heard and supported.

I warmly invite you to visit our campus, meet our teachers and experience the EDUCARE spirit for yourself.

Meera Krishnan

Dr. Meera Krishnan

Principal · Ph.D. Education (University of Hyderabad), M.Ed. · 26 years in education leadership
