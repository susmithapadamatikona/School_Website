Admission Process

Your step-by-step admission guide

From registration to your child's first day — here is exactly what to expect.

Start Application

Step 1: Online Registration Day 1

Create a parent account and complete the short enquiry form on our website.

  • Choose the grade you are applying for
  • Receive an Application ID instantly
  • Registration fee: ₹1,500 (non-refundable)

Step 2: Application Submission Day 1–3

Fill in student, parent and address details and upload the required documents.

  • Save progress as a draft anytime
  • Upload birth certificate, report card and photo
  • Review every detail before submission

Step 3: Document Verification Within 3 working days

Our admissions office verifies documents and may request clarifications.

  • Status updates in real time
  • Originals verified during the campus visit

Step 4: Entrance Assessment Scheduled slot

Age-appropriate assessment in English, Mathematics and reasoning (Grade 2 onwards).

  • 60–90 minutes on campus
  • Informal interaction for Pre-Primary
  • Results shared within a week

Step 5: Parent Interaction Scheduled slot

Meet the Principal and coordinators to understand our philosophy and your expectations.

  • Campus tour included
  • Q&A with section coordinators

Step 6: Admission Confirmation Within 14 days of offer

Accept the offer and pay the admission fee to confirm the seat.

  • Online payment via parent portal
  • Orientation kit & timetable shared
  • Student and parent portal access activated
