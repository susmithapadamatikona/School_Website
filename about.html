Our Story

Built by educators, for learners

EDUCARE was founded by Mr. Anand Rao and a group of teachers who believed that a school should feel like a second home — rigorous, joyful and deeply human.

Today our 22-acre Gachibowli campus welcomes students from 28 nationalities. We follow the CBSE curriculum enriched with Cambridge Primary, STEM and design-thinking programmes, and we measure success not just in marks but in character, creativity and compassion.

  • CBSE affiliated since 2003
  • Cambridge partner school
  • Green School Platinum award
  • ISO 9001:2015 certified
  • Smart classrooms throughout
  • Full-time counsellors & nurses
